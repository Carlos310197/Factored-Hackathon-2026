## Human ceiling
27/40 = 0.675 (blind, after the test run)

Method and reading: the rater answered `none` whenever a message was ambiguous, instead of guessing. Of the 13 misses, 11 were `none` answers on cases whose target was in the list (an abstention), 1 named the wrong candidate and 1 picked a candidate on a not-in-list case. Among the 27 answers that named a candidate, 25 were right (92.6%). So 0.675 is a floor set by the abstain-on-ambiguity rule, not the rate at which a person can find the transaction; it is not comparable to `resolved_one_step`, which counts a good question to the customer as resolved.

## Limitations
- Training mentions are simulated; the style rates are assumptions (`simulate.yaml`).
- Portuguese messages describe the histories of Spanish-speaking customers; country is the customer's most frequent
  transaction country (histories carry no customer attributes).
- Slices marked ⚠ have fewer than 30 cases and are not conclusive. Zero observed errors does not mean zero risk.
